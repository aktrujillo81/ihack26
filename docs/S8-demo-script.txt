WELL WATCH — 90-SECOND DEMO SCRIPT

SETUP BEFORE STARTING

- Open the responder dashboard on the projector.
- Sign in as a Responder before the judges arrive.
- Keep the phone number visible for the judge.
- Have the Viewer account ready in a second browser or private window.
- Confirm the phone line, database, and dashboard are working.

0:00–0:10 — OPENING

Say:
“A broken water point can leave a community without water before an
organization knows there is a problem. Well Watch turns a short phone call
into a report an NGO coordinator can see, prioritize, and follow up on.”

0:10–0:15 — SHOW THE DASHBOARD

Say:
“This is the NGO coordinator dashboard. It shows each active complaint, its
priority, why it received that priority, its status, and whether it needs
human review.”

0:15–0:45 — JUDGE CALLS

Invite the judge to call the number and say:
“I am calling about a hand pump in Riverside village, near the market. There
is no water. About one hundred and fifty people use it. A school uses it too.
It has been a week.”

While the call runs, say:
“The assistant asks one question at a time, confirms the important details,
and does not ask for the caller’s name or phone number. It does not give water
safety advice or promise a repair.”

0:45–0:55 — SHOW THE NEW COMPLAINT

When the complaint appears, say:
“The report now appears here. The priority is explained rather than hidden:
there is no water, many people are affected, a school depends on it, and it
has been broken for a week.”

If it is a new water point, say:
“Because this is a new location, it is marked unverified and appears in Needs
location instead of being silently placed on the map.”

0:55–1:05 — VERIFY LOCATION AND MAP

Open the complaint and place or show the map pin.

Say:
“A responder confirms the location. The point is now visible on the map as an
open problem. The label and shape communicate the status too, so color is not
the only signal.”

1:05–1:17 — RECORD THE HAND-OFF

Choose a seeded partner and add a short note.

Say:
“The coordinator records that the complaint was passed on to a partner. This
is a tracked hand-off, not an automatic dispatch and not a message sent by the
system.”

1:17–1:25 — MARK FIXED

Mark the complaint fixed.

Say:
“Once the repair is confirmed, the coordinator marks it fixed. The map status
changes and the timeline keeps every step.”

1:25–1:30 — SHOW PERMISSIONS AND CLOSE

Show the same complaint while signed in as a Viewer, or show the disabled and
denied edit state.

Say:
“A Viewer can see reports in their region but cannot change them. Well Watch
keeps people in control while making reports visible and actionable.”


FALLBACK IF THE LIVE CALL FAILS

Say:
“We have a recorded backup of this same end-to-end flow.”

Then play the backup video and point out: phone report, new complaint,
priority reason, needs-location state, recorded hand-off, fixed status, and
Viewer edit denial.


DEMO GUARDRAILS

- Do not say a repair is guaranteed or scheduled.
- Do not say water is safe or unsafe.
- Do not display caller names, phone numbers, raw payloads, or secrets.
- Do not claim a partner received a message; say the hand-off was recorded.
- If the location is ambiguous, say the report is honestly marked Needs review.
